- Lockup raids: see above.

- Decoy cars (two cars, fake in one trunk): the Getaway kit text explains it; an accomplishment ("Wrong Van") would push it but adds an eleventh feat; hold until Studio shows decoys are used at all.

## Where boredom is likely
1. Between museum restocks (600 s cycle) when no convoy is scheduled: raids need Runner; a Drifter can only wait. Candidate: let the restock cycle shorten when the museum was fully cleared (already 90 s after the vault empties) and make Ines say how long until the next stock.
2. Waiting for a convoy with nobody wealthy to raid: Teo's bucketed ETA is the mitigation; the auto schedule jitter (480–720 s after a 900 s cooldown) may be too long for small servers. Tune from Studio session logs, not now.
3. Solo players at night with high heat: the only pressure release is the jail or waiting; fine as tension if cooldowns are short enough.
